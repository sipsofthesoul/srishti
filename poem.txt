There is something about her
that winter could never quite explain.
Something the snow has tried to capture
and the moon has spent centuries
trying to imitate.

She carries the kind of beauty
that makes the world seem quieter,
like even the stars
have somewhere better to look.

And yet,
my favourite things about her
have very little to do with beauty.

It's the endless yapping,
the gossip that somehow becomes breaking news,
the wonderfully unnecessary details
that could fill entire libraries.

It's the occasional stupidity,
the stubbornness,
the little ways she gets annoying
without even trying.

Somehow, those are the hours
I find myself looking forward to most.
The ordinary parts of my day
become something worth remembering
simply because she was in them.

I could listen to her talk
about absolutely nothing
and still feel
like I was being told everything.

She has made a home
out of the smallest moments,
out of passing conversations,
unimportant stories,
and laughter that stays
long after the call has ended.

I don't think she understands
how much space she occupies
in a life that once knew
how to exist without her.

If winter ever lost its snow,
if the moon ever misplaced its light,
there would still be something beautiful
left for the world to look at.

Her.

And if time is generous enough
to grant me anything,
I hope it grants me the ordinary things
with her.

More pointless conversations.
More ridiculous arguments.
More stories I've already heard.
More days where her voice
is the highlight of everything.

I don't just want her
for the beautiful moments.

I want the unremarkable Tuesdays,
the tired evenings,
the silences, the nonsense,
and all the little things
that make a life a life.

I want to keep finding her
in every version of tomorrow,
until forever stops sounding
like a promise
and starts looking
like a lifetime spent beside her.
